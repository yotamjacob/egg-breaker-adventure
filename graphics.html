💡 TikTok Content Ideas

Quick tips for TikTok
  • Film in 9:16 vertical — crop screenshots to phone aspect ratio before posting
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds — show a satisfying egg smash, a rare drop, or a Starfall event
  • Use the monkey portraits as overlays or reaction images in CapCut
  • Trending sounds + gameplay = free reach — mute the game audio and overlay music
🥚
Egg Opening Montage

Screen record a Starfall event or a run of Century Eggs. Fast-cut to a trending sound. The item pop-up animations are very satisfying on camera.

EASY · HIGH VIEWS
🐵
Pick Your Monkey

Show all 6 monkey portraits one by one and ask viewers to comment their favourite. Drives comments → algorithm boost.

ENGAGEMENT
📖
The Origin Story

"This Facebook game disappeared in 2016 — so I rebuilt it myself." Use the history from the press kit. Solo dev stories perform very well.

STORY · VIRAL POTENTIAL
🔨
Hammer Tier List

Show each special hammer (Mjölnir, Rainbow, Crystal…) and rank them. Tier list videos consistently perform in gaming niches.

GAMING FORMAT
🏆
Hidden Achievements

Reveal a secret achievement or Easter egg. "Most players don't know this exists" hooks people immediately.

SECRETS · HOOK
📵
No Ads. Ever.

"I made a mobile game with zero ads" is a viral format right now. Lead with the tagline, show 30 seconds of clean gameplay.

ANTI-AD ANGLE · TRENDING
🌍
World Tour

Show each monkey's world one at a time — Mr. Monkey → Steampunk → Princess → Space → Odin → Wukong. "6 worlds, 0 paywalls."

SHOWCASE
🎩
Hat Try-On

Flip through all 6 hat variants on one monkey. Costume / cosmetics videos do well — ask "which hat is best?" in the caption.

AESTHETIC · COMMENTS
🇮🇱
Solo Dev Devlog

30-second "built this game alone from Israel" devlog. Show code, show the game, show yourself. Dev content has its own loyal niche.
