About the app

Egg Smash Adventures

Egg Smash Adventures is a free egg-smashing collection game for your browser and for Android. You spend hammers to crack eggs, win gold, star pieces and rare items, fill collections stage by stage, and unlock six monkey companions along the way. It is a fan revival of the 2008 Facebook game Egg Breaker, rebuilt from scratch.

▶ Play in your browser Get it on Google Play

What the app does

Every round puts a few eggs on the tray. Tapping an egg costs one hammer; when it cracks it pays out a prize — gold, multiplier chips, star pieces, feathers or one of 353 collectible items. Complete a stage's collection to unlock the next stage, complete a monkey's nine stages to unlock the next monkey. Hammers refill over time, so the game plays in short sessions and rewards coming back.

  • 6 monkeys, 53 stages, 353 items, 123 trophies — plus daily and weekly quests.
  • Shop & upgrades paid with in-game gold: hammers that level up as you use them, hats, skills, an Auto-Smasher that keeps playing while you are away.
  • Free to play. No ads. A handful of optional premium items exist on Android; nothing in the game is locked behind them.
  • Works offline in the browser once loaded, and on any phone, tablet or desktop.

Why Egg Smash Adventures asks for Google user data

Progress is saved on your device automatically. Optionally, you can sign in with Google (Settings → Cloud Save) to back your save up and carry it between your phone and your browser. That is the only reason the app requests Google user data.

  • What we request: your basic profile — email address and Google account ID — via the openid, email and profile scopes. Nothing else: no contacts, no Drive, no Gmail, no calendar.
  • What it is used for: identifying your account so your game save can be stored and restored. Your email is not shown to other players, not used for marketing, and never sold.
  • What we never do: post on your behalf, read your data from other Google services, or share your data with third parties beyond the hosting providers named in the privacy policy.
  • Your control: unlink Google at any time from the same screen; you can also delete your cloud save there. The game is fully playable without ever signing in.
