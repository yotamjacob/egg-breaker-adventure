How to start playing

  1. Open the game. It loads straight into the egg tray — there is no account wall and no tutorial to sit through.
  2. Tap an egg. Each smash spends one hammer and rolls a prize: gold, a multiplier, a bonus, or a collection item.
  3. Fill the collection. Every stage has its own item set. Complete it and the next stage unlocks.
  4. Come back later. Hammers regenerate on their own, so you are never blocked for long — and never nagged.

What the game is

You break eggs with a hammer. Each egg rolls a prize from its own table, and the rarer the egg, the better the table. Most prizes are gold and multipliers; the ones you actually want are collection items, which fill the album stage by stage.

  • 6 monkey companions — each unlocks a themed world with its own stages, items and passive bonus.
  • 8–9 stages per monkey and 353 collectible items in total.
  • 7 egg tiers — Normal, Silver, Gold, Crystal, Ruby, Black and the mythical Century Egg.
  • Multipliers and Starfall events for stacking a single enormous payout.
  • 3 active skills — Monkey Rage, Golden Goose and Banana Shake.
  • Hats and hammers to collect, for stat bonuses and pure cosmetics.
  • Daily rewards with a streak that actually escalates.

New to it? The full guide walks through monkeys, egg tiers and how collections work. Wondering where the original went? That story is here.

Does it work on my device?

If it runs a modern browser, yes. The game is plain HTML5 and works on desktop Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, plus mobile browsers on iOS and Android. There is no plugin to install — Flash has been dead since 2020 and nothing here depends on it.

On iPhone or iPad you can add it to your home screen from the Safari share menu and it behaves like an app, fullscreen and offline-capable. On Android you can do the same, or install the Play Store build.
