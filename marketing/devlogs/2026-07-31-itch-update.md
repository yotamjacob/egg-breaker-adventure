```
Hi — first devlog here, so let me start with why this game exists, then what's new.

## The game this is based on doesn't exist anymore

Somewhere around 2008 there was a Facebook game called Egg Breaker. You spent a
hammer, cracked an egg, and out came gold, a multiplier, or — if you were lucky —
a collection item you didn't have yet. Fill the collection, unlock the next
stage. That was the whole thing, and it was oddly hard to put down.

It was retired around 2016 when the studio wound down its Facebook titles. No
announcement, no sequel. Whatever survived was finished off when browsers dropped
Flash at the end of 2020. If you go looking for it now you find nothing —
Facebook app pages don't leave an archive behind, and "egg breaker" is a generic
enough phrase that real memories of it get buried under kitchen gadgets and
Easter apps.

I played it constantly as a kid. I assumed someone would eventually remake it.
Nobody did, so I built this.

It's a spiritual successor, not a copy — the art, the worlds and every item are
new. What's faithful is the rhythm: crack, roll, collect, and get slightly too
invested in finishing a set.

## What's new in this build

The itch version had drifted a few versions behind the web and Android releases.
It's now identical to what's live everywhere else, plus two things you can
actually see:

**Share your progress.** Settings → Share Your Progress builds a link with the
stage you reached baked into it. Whoever opens it gets a "your friend reached
Stage 7 — can you beat it?" greeting and drops straight into the game. No signup
on either end.

**A real guide.** Settings → Game Guide opens a proper writeup: all seven egg
tiers and what each is actually worth, every hat and hammer with real numbers,
how stage tiers and collections gate progress, and the handful of decisions that
genuinely matter early. If you've been guessing at whether the Spyglass is worth
5,000 gold — it is, and now there's a page explaining why.

There's also a "The Story" link in there if you want the longer version of the
history above.

## What it is, if you're new

- Six monkey companions, each with their own themed world and a passive bonus
- 8–9 stages each, **353 collectible items** in total
- Seven egg tiers, from the everyday Normal egg up to the 100-hit Century Egg
- Nine hammers and five hats that change real numbers, not just looks
- Starfall bonus rounds and stacking multipliers
- **No ads, no energy timers, no paywalls.** Hammers regenerate on their own.

Plays free right here in the browser. There's an Android build too if you'd
rather have it on your home screen with cloud save, but the browser version is
the full game — not a demo.

## Feedback wanted

Genuinely: what makes you stop playing? Whether it's a difficulty wall, a stage
that drags, or a collection that felt unfair — that's the most useful thing you
can tell me. Comments are open.
```
