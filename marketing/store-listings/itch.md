## Description

```
Smash eggs. Win prizes. Finish the album.

A free browser revival of Egg Breaker, the Facebook game from around 2008 that
vanished when Flash did. Spend a hammer, crack an egg, get gold, a multiplier or
a collection item you didn't have yet. Fill the collection, unlock the next
stage. It was oddly hard to put down then, and it still is.

## What's in it

- 6 monkey companions, each with its own world of stages and a perk that
  changes how you play — 53 stages and 353 collectible items in total.
- Seven egg types, from the everyday Normal egg to the 100-hit Century Egg.
  Rarer eggs take more hits and pay out far more.
- Egg effects: runny eggs that slide, timed eggs that vanish, hexed eggs, balloon
  eggs you long-press for 10×, and teleporting silver and gold eggs that beam
  away when you hit them — catch one for 4× the prize.
- Multipliers, star pieces and Starfall — a rain of stars that clears the tray.
- Three skills (Monkey Rage, Golden Goose, Banana Shake), each upgradeable.

## It plays while you're away

Unlock the Auto-Smasher in the shop and it keeps cracking eggs after you close
the tab. Come back to a report — eggs, gold, star pieces, items — and a full
hammer bar. Upgrade its speed, how long it covers, and how much it keeps.

## Something to do every day

Five daily quests and three weekly ones, the same for everyone that day, paid in
gold, feathers, star pieces and max hammers. Daily login rewards with a 30-day
streak on top.

## Hammers that grow with you

Eight special hammers, five hats, permanent upgrades — all bought with in-game
gold. The hammer you have equipped earns XP as you play, up to level 10: its
effect grows, level 5 gives free hits, level 10 unlocks a perk unique to that
hammer. 123 trophies track all of it, some of them secret.

## Free, no ads, no energy walls

Hammers regenerate on their own. No interstitials, no rewarded videos, nothing
locked. Your progress saves in the browser; sign in with Google (optional) and
it syncs to the cloud and to the Android version.

Also on Android — the same game with cloud save between phone and browser.

## Controls

Click or tap an egg to hit it. Tap the stage bar to switch stages, the gold
counter to jump to the shop, the gear for settings, sound and music.

Made by one person, no engine, vanilla JavaScript. This is an unofficial fan
revival and isn't affiliated with the makers of the original. If you played
Egg Breaker back then and something feels off, say so in the comments — a fair
few features started as someone's comment.
```
