```
Egg Smash Adventures is a rebuild of Egg Breaker, the Facebook game from around 2008 that disappeared when Flash did. If you played it back then, this is that loop: spend a hammer, crack an egg, get gold, a multiplier or a collection item, fill the collection, move to the next stage. If you didn't, it's a collectathon idle game where every egg is a small gamble and the album is the long game.

What is in it

- 6 monkeys, each with a different perk (Steampunk finds more star pieces, Princess more gold, Space Cadette more items, and so on). You unlock them by finishing stages.
- 53 stages across the six monkeys, each with its own collection of items to complete.
- 353 collectible items in the album, in common, uncommon and rare tiers. Rare pulls are genuinely rare.
- Seven egg types: normal, silver, gold, crystal, ruby, black and century. Higher tiers pay out much bigger and stop giving empty prizes, but take more hits to crack.
- Multipliers, star pieces and Starfall (seven star pieces triggers a rain of gold across the whole tray).
- Three skills that unlock after your second monkey: Monkey Rage, Golden Goose and Banana Shake, each upgradeable.
- A shop with hammers, hats, upgrades and consumables, daily login rewards, and 109 in-game trophies (seven of them secret).
- 12 Newgrounds medals and 3 scoreboards (total gold, eggs smashed, stages completed). Log in to Newgrounds and they track automatically.

Notes for this version

This build was made for Newgrounds specifically. Hammers regenerate every few seconds instead of on a mobile-style timer, so you can play a full session without hitting a wall. There are no store links, no ads and nothing to buy in this version. Progress saves locally in your browser.

The game also runs on the web and Android, and cloud save can move progress between them; that is optional and not needed to play here.

Controls

Click or tap an egg to hit it. Tap the stage bar to switch stages, the gold counter to jump to the shop, and the gear for settings, sound and music.

Background

Egg Breaker was a small, oddly compelling game and there is nowhere left to play it. I rebuilt it from memory and from what people who played it could still recall, then kept adding: more monkeys, more stages, the skills, the trophies. It is one person's project. If you remember the original and something feels off, or you have found a bug, say so in the reviews and I will fix it. If you played the original at all I would like to hear about it.

Made with vanilla JavaScript, no engine.
```
