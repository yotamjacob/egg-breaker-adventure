# Google Ads App Campaign — text assets

Paste into Google Ads → App campaign → Ad assets. Limits: headlines ≤30 chars, descriptions ≤90 chars.
Order does not matter; Google mixes and tests combinations. Keep at least 5 of each.

## Campaign settings (first run)

- Type App → App installs → Google Play → Egg Smash Adventures
- Bidding: Install volume, all users. Switch to in-app actions only after ~50+ purchases are recorded.
- Locations: US, UK, CA, AU + BR, PH as low-CPI comparison markets. Language: English.
- Budget: $10–15/day, target CPI ~$1. Do not edit for 14 days (learning phase).
- Conversions: link Play Console → import First open + In-app purchases (no SDK needed).
- Read after 14 days: CPI by country, D1 retention of the paid cohort (Play Console), purchases from paid installs.
