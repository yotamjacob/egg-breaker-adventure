The core loop

You spend hammers to hit eggs. Tougher eggs take more hits — a Normal egg breaks in one, a Century Egg takes a hundred. When an egg finally cracks it rolls a prize from its own table: gold, a multiplier, star pieces, feathers, bonus hammers, or a collection item.

Collection items are the only prize that moves you forward. Fill a stage's collection and the next stage unlocks. Everything else — gold, hats, better hammers — exists to make items arrive faster.

Hammers regenerate on a timer, so a session ends naturally rather than with a paywall. You start with 75.

Egg tiers

Seven tiers. Rarer eggs take more hits but roll from strictly better tables — and the top tiers cannot come up empty at all.

🪨 Silver2Stage 12× gold, rarely empty, and the first egg that can drop bonus hammers.
🌟 Gold3Stage 1Never empty, 1.5× gold, and roughly double a Normal egg's item chance.
🔮 Crystal4Stage 33× gold and 4 star pieces per crack — the fastest route to Starfall.
💎 Ruby9Stage 56× gold, 5 star pieces, and a much higher item rate.
🖤 Black20Stage 910× gold, 7 star pieces, and the only egg that raises your max hammers.
🌀 Century100Complete Mr. Monkey100× everything. A hundred hammers for one crack — and worth it.

Rare eggs spawn on their own; you cannot buy one. What you can do is make sure you see them — the Spyglass upgrade (5,000 gold) labels each egg with its type and remaining hits, which stops you from dumping hammers into a Ruby you did not plan for.

Collections & stage progress

Each stage has its own item set — Tropical Fruits, Sea Creatures, Crown Jewels and so on. Items come in Common, Uncommon and Rare, and duplicates convert to gold rather than being wasted.

Milestones pay out as you fill a stage:

Collection filledWhat you get
40%Hammer refill
70%Next stage unlocks, plus max hammers and a refill
100%Stage complete: crystal banana and a bigger refill

Completing a stage pays out bonus max hammers and a refill, which compounds: finishing stages properly makes every later stage cheaper to grind.

Gold, star pieces, feathers & bananas

  • 🪙 Gold — the main currency. Buys hammers, hats, hammers upgrades and permanent shop upgrades.
  • ⭐ Star pieces — collect 7 to trigger a Starfall, a bonus round that pays out across the whole tray.
  • 🪶 Feathers — the anti-frustration currency. Feathers buy a specific missing item outright: 5 for a Common, 12 for an Uncommon, 30 for a Rare (rising slightly per stage). When one item is blocking a collection, this is the fix.
  • 🍌 Crystal bananas — earned by completing stages and spent on unlocking new monkeys (9 per monkey).

Auto-Smasher (idle play)

A gold-shop upgrade that unlocks once Mr. Monkey reaches stage 3 (15,000 gold). It taps eggs for you using your hammers — a robot button in the tray toggles it — and it keeps working while the game is closed. Come back and a "While you were away" report shows what it smashed: gold, star pieces, feathers, multipliers and up to three new collection items per absence (never a Rare — those you find yourself).

Because it spends hammers, idle income can never exceed what regen would have paid you anyway. Three upgrade tracks:

UpgradeLevelsWhat it does
⏱️ Smasher Speed20s → 12s → 8s → 5s → 3s per tapHow fast it taps, online and away
🌙 Away Time2h → 4h → 8h → 12h → 24hHow much of an absence is simulated
💰 Away Efficiency50% → 65% → 80% → 100%Share of away gold you keep

Away time beyond the cap still regenerates hammers normally, so a long break is never wasted. Six trophies track unlocking and upgrading it.

Tips that actually matter

  1. Buy the Spyglass early. At 5,000 gold it is the cheapest upgrade that changes decisions, because you can finally see what you are hitting.
  2. Save feathers for the last item in a set. Feathers scale with rarity, but the real cost of a missing Rare is the hundreds of hammers you would otherwise spend fishing for it.
  3. Stack multipliers before a rare egg, not after. The multiplier applies to the prize roll, so spend them where the table is best.
  4. Finish stages completely, not just to "unlocked". The completion reward is max hammers — a permanent increase to how much you can play per session.
  5. Do not ignore duplicates. They convert to gold scaled by rarity, so a duplicate Rare is a meaningful payout, not a miss.
  6. Switch monkeys when you stall. Bananas from a fresh world buy the unlock for a better bonus, and progress on the old one is kept exactly where you left it.
